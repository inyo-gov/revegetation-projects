Inyo County Water Department

Author

Inyo County Water Department

Published

Scope of this page

What: 2025 line-point resurvey of 1991 EIR / 1999 Revegetation Plan mitigation parcels—cover and composition against Table 2 goals (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power 1999).

Why: Separate from Laws Type E (2003). Partners need a transparent path from segment hits → site goals. Pages marked (draft) elsewhere are prototypes; this report is the working 1999-plan monitoring product.

Purpose

Parcels: IND105, IND123, IND131N/S, BLK16E, TIN054, BGP160E/W, BIS097, and LAW118 (1999-plan context)—not the Type E transfer dashboard (Laws / reference).

Cover math: We use species hit counts only from the 2025 workbook; staff %COV / %COMP columns are ignored. Percent cover = hits / n_possible_hits × 100, with n_possible_hits = segment length (m) / 0.5.1

Goal outcomes: Table 1 · map: Fig. 6.

1999 Revegetation Plan goals

What the Plan requires (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power 1999): site-specific live native perennial cover, composition (species contributing to cover), and recruitment / sustainability (≥25% of vegetation cover as natural recruits ≥3 years old).

What this report scores: cover and composition from 2025 LPI (with the master-workbook CI rules below). Sustainability is not scored from LPI alone here; the LADWP aerial pilot is summarized next as context for that third criterion.

Goals below follow Table 2 (2021–22 Mitigation Projects and Status).

LADWP proposal — aerial recruitment / sustainability (2026)

What: A remote-sensing workflow (high-res imagery + LiDAR → live/dead/bare → interannual overlap) to estimate recruit share of live cover on IND105.

Why summarize it here: The Plan’s 25% recruit rule needs an operational definition; this pilot is the current LA proposal. Full deck remains local (reveg1999plan-docs/); County dual-track notes in docs/1999plan_sustainability_monitoring_proposal.md.

Why IND105: cover and composition already met—useful for testing sustainability; IND123 is also of interest for full completion.

Workflow (slides 7–8)

  1. Establish “current” vegetation from 0.05 m imagery + LiDAR (August 2022).
  2. Georeference (spline).
  3. Object recognition (eCognition).
  4. Random Forest → Bare Ground / Live Vegetation / Dead Vegetation.
  5. Merge and explode live polygons.
  6. Superimpose year-of-interest live polygons on a prior year.
  7. New recruits = no overlap with prior live cover, or overlapping area < 10% of the polygon (also: previously undetectable, < 0.3 m).

Recruitment metric and pilot scores

What: (R) = (recruit live area) / (total live vegetation cover), compared to Plan ≥25% of vegetation cover—not parcel area (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power 1999).

Epoch Live cover Recruit cover Recruit % of cover vs 25%
2014 vs 2009 14.0% 1.93% 13.7% below
2017 vs 2014 (unadjusted) 38.9% 17.0% 43.8% above
2017 adjusted (×0.74 + persist to 2022) 28.9% 14.4% 49.7% above
2022 vs 2017 11.1% 1.27% 11.5% below

Annual issue (slide 13): 2017 RS cannot separate annuals; IND106 T10/T12 (within IND105) had 25.7% annual hits → scale cover × 0.743; keep non-intersecting polygons that overlap 2022 live or dead.

Caveats from LA watershed resources staff: Annuals inflate recruitment area. If credited as recruitment in 2017 but empty in 2022, drop from the 25% share (annual signal; perennial mortality is an alternate assumption). Lateral growth ≠ recruitment on LPI alone; sample ≠ whole site without a sampling frame.

Recommendations (slide 17)

  1. Acquire high-resolution remote sensing (~0.05 m / 5 cm) with LiDAR every three to five years. (Deck text says “< 0.05 cm” — treat as typo for 0.05 m, matching the 2022 acquisition.)
  2. Conduct ground monitoring concurrently with RS acquisition.

Going forward (ICWD): treat 2022 as baseline; compare expected 2024 3DEP LiDAR to 2022 and to 2025 transects; refine baseline before the next high-res flight.

Compliance method (from LADWP 1991 EIR REVEGETATION DATA-MASTER.xlsm): cover is met when the upper 80% confidence interval of mean transect cover reaches 90% of the Table 2 cover goal (upper CI80 ≥ 0.9 × goal). Species composition is met when observed richness reaches 75% of the Table 2 species goal. The 80% CI uses mean + t₀.₉,ₙ₋₁ × SD / √n across transects (or segments, for 2025).

This report compares 2025 live native perennial cover, upper 80% CI, and native perennial species with ≥3 hits to those targets. Cover and species metrics use the same perennial filter as the LADWP master workbook (Lifecycle contains “Perennial”). Parcels with missing segment lengths (denominators) are marked incomplete.

Parcel boundaries: AGOL polygons only — no transect buffering. Most parcels use mitigation_sites.shp; LAW118 uses mitigation_view_polygons.geojson; TIN054 uses the experimental plot in data/gis/TIN054.json. TIN054 transect geometry is TBD (current post pairing draws incorrectly; map left blank until remapped).

```{r load-data}
library(dplyr)
library(DT)
library(leaflet)
library(sf)
library(readr)
library(knitr)
library(ggplot2)
library(ggrepel)

source("code/parse_reveg1999plan_workbook.R")
source("code/parse_reveg1999_master_workbook.R")
source("code/reveg1999plan_maps.R")

data1999 <- load_reveg1999plan_data()
write_reveg1999plan_outputs(data1999)

master_data <- load_reveg1999_master_data()
write_reveg1999_master_outputs(master_data)

hits <- data1999$hits
meta <- data1999$metadata
species_attr <- read_csv("data/species.csv", show_col_types = FALSE)

segment_cover <- summarise_reveg1999_segment_cover(hits, species_attr)
posts_sf <- load_reveg1999_transect_posts()
segment_points <- build_reveg1999_segment_points(segment_cover, posts_sf)
segment_lines <- build_reveg1999_segment_lines(segment_cover, posts_sf)
boundaries <- load_reveg1999_parcel_boundaries()

missing_by_parcel <- hits |>
  group_by(parcel) |>
  summarise(
    n_segment_keys = n_distinct(segment_key),
    segments_missing_denom = n_distinct(segment_key[is.na(n_possible_hits)]),
    .groups = "drop"
  )

parcel_metrics_calc <- compute_reveg1999_parcel_metrics(hits, species_attr) |>
  fix_parcel_metrics_denominators(hits)

parcel_metrics <- tibble(parcel = sort(unique(hits$parcel))) |>
  left_join(REVEG1999_PARCEL_GOALS, by = "parcel") |>
  left_join(meta |> select(parcel, survey_date), by = "parcel") |>
  left_join(missing_by_parcel, by = "parcel") |>
  left_join(parcel_metrics_calc, by = "parcel", suffix = c("", "_calc")) |>
  mutate(
    assessment_complete = if_else(
      is.na(assessment_complete),
      segments_missing_denom == 0L,
      assessment_complete
    )
  ) |>
  arrange(parcel)

parcel_list <- parcel_metrics$parcel
```
